# DARPA Quantum Programs

## Quantum Benchmarking Initiative (QBI)
- **Stage A**: Demonstrate quantum advantage on a useful problem (2024-2026)
- **Stage B**: Scale to 100+ logical qubits (2026-2028)
- **Stage C**: Fault-tolerant quantum computing (2028-2030)
- **Participants**: IonQ, IBM, Quantinuum, Microsoft, others
- **Trading relevance**: Stage transitions are major catalysts for participants

## Underexplored Systems for Utility-Scale Quantum Computing (US2QC)
- **Goal**: Identify new qubit modalities that could leapfrog current approaches
- **Participants**: Various academic and industry teams
- **Status**: Phase 1 evaluations ongoing

## ONISQ (Optimization with Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum devices)
- **Goal**: Prove quantum advantage for optimization problems
- **Relevance to D-Wave**: Annealing-based approaches directly targeted

## Quantum Network Technology
- **Goal**: Build entanglement-based quantum networks
- **Relevance**: Infrastructure play; benefits networking-focused companies

## Key Dates for Investors
- **QBI Stage A reviews**: Annually, typically Q3/Q4
- **Contract award announcements**: Watch sam.gov and DARPA news page
- **Budget cycles**: DoD budget proposals (Feb) signal funding direction
